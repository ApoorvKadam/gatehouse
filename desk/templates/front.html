{% include "icons/scale.svg" %}Lien files

A lien file goes back to review when the account has changed.

For a Florida unit, every requirement of the lien statute is listed with its basis and the evidence recorded for it, and a missing record is an open item. A file is evaluated when a person asks. A payment on or after the day the notice was delivered sends the file back to a person at that evaluation instead of on to the sale; a payment of the whole balance takes it out of lien work. The excerpt shows the latest evaluation as it was recorded. Gatehouse never says a unit may be sold.
