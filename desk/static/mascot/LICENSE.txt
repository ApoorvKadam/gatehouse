The keeper (guard-directions.webp, guard-reactions.webp) is an original character drawn for Gatehouse with the page-mascot
skill, https://github.com/nilbuild/page-mascot: nine head turns and nine faces, built into aligned sheets by the skill's own
build and checked to move 0 px between them. The pointer-following script in desk/templates/base.html follows that project's
component, which is under the MIT licence below.
